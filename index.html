How to use this program (Step-by-Step)

Click to toggle

Phase 1: The LEADER (6 Weeks)

  • Step 1: Enter 1RMs and click "INITIAL SETUP: GENERATE TMs". Ensure "Leader" is active.
  • Step 2: Complete Week 1, 2, and 3 workouts.
  • Step 3: Click "Complete Cycle (+kg)". Your weights increase.
  • Step 4: Complete Week 1, 2, and 3 again (Cycle 2).

Phase 2: The DELOAD (1 Week)

  • Step 5: Click "Complete Cycle (+kg)" again.
  • Step 6: Click the "7th Week Protocol" tab. Select "Deload". Do the light workouts.

Phase 3: The ANCHOR (3 Weeks)

  • Step 7: Click the "Anchor" button at the top.
  • Step 8: Complete Weeks 1, 2, and 3. Push for PRs on main sets.

Phase 4: The TM TEST & RESET (1 Week)

  • Step 9: Click "Complete Cycle (+kg)".
  • Step 10: Go to "7th Week Protocol" tab. Select "TM Test".
  • Step 11: Perform your test. Enter your reps. The app will calculate your EXACT new TM based on performance AND update your true 1RM box.
  • Step 12: Record your new True 1RM, switch back to "Leader" and click the gray "Start New Block (Cycle 1)" button.
